Our Programs

Discover the impactful initiatives we run to empower Samburu women and communities.

Human rights protection, research and advocacy

Objective: Increase school enrollment and retention for girls in remote Samburu areas.

  • Full scholarships for 100 girls annually
  • Mentorship programs with female role models
  • School supplies and sanitary towels distribution
  • Parental engagement and community awareness
Support this program

women in climate change, land and natural resource governance

Objective: Reduce maternal and infant mortality through improved healthcare access.

  • Mobile health clinics reaching 15 remote villages
  • Training traditional birth attendants
  • Prenatal and postnatal care
  • Family planning education
Support this program

Indigenous women livelihoods and economic empowerment

Objective: Foster economic independence through skills development and micro-enterprise.

  • Micro-loans and grants for women entrepreneurs
  • Beadwork cooperatives and market linkages
  • Financial literacy workshops
  • Business mentorship and networking
