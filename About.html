About Naapu Fund

NAAPU exists to strengthen Indigenous women’s leadership, visibility, and access to resources so their work can grow and sustain communities.

Why NAAPU Exists

Across Africa and globally, Indigenous women remain among the least funded despite being at the forefront of climate adaptation, biodiversity protection, peacebuilding, food systems, cultural preservation, and community resilience.

Barriers Indigenous Women Face

  • Complex donor compliance systems.
  • Limited access to formal registration for Indigenous communities.
  • Geographic marginalisation and digital exclusion.
  • Language and literacy barriers.
  • Poor policy inclusion and formulation for women from Indigenous communities.
  • Restricted and short-term funding models.
  • Exclusion from decision-making spaces.
  • Limited recognition of Indigenous knowledge systems.

Our Values

  • Integrity: Transparent and accountable operations.
  • Community: Working hand-in-hand with local leaders.
  • Resilience: Building sustainable solutions.
  • Cultural Respect: Preserving Samburu heritage.

Why NAAPU Fund

The NAAPU Fund was established from a simple but powerful belief: Indigenous women are not victims waiting to be saved — they are leaders, solution builders, custodians of Indigenous knowledge, defenders of territories, and protectors of ecosystems and future generations.

Core and Flexible Funding

Rigid donor frameworks often prevent Indigenous women groups from responding to urgent community priorities, building grassroots-led innovations, and growing sustainable institutions and movements.

NAAPU believes that trust-based philanthropy begins by trusting Indigenous women to identify their own priorities, adapt to changing realities, and allocate resources where they are needed.

What Core Funding Supports

  • Sustain day-to-day operations.
  • Retain staff and community organisers.
  • Strengthen community presence and outreach.
  • Respond to emergency crises and opportunities.
  • Build long-term development strategies and sustainability.
  • Invest in Indigenous-led movement building and leadership development.
  • Strengthen accountability and gender-inclusive governance systems.
  • Maintain safe spaces for women and girls.
