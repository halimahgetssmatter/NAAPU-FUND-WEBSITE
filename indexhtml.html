NAAPU Fund

NAAPU means to"Rise" or "uplift". NAAPU Indigenous fund is a feminist indigenous women-led, trust based funding initiative that places resources directly into the hands of grassroot indigenous women.

About Naapu Fund

WHY NAAPU MATTERS

Indigenous women are the leading change agents in their communities- advancing gender justice, protecting land and culture, responding to climate shocks and supporting women and girls facing violence and marginalisation.

WE FUND AND SUPPORT

A future where every Indigenous woman thrives with dignity, self-reliance, and equal participation in community development.
